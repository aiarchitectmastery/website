Ari Vilkman — AI Architect

Ari Vilkman is an AI Architect and the founder of AI Architect Mastery, where he created the AI Driven Development Methodology: a human-led way to build software with AI agents through four phases, PRD → PLAN → TASK → IMPLEMENTATION. He is also the founder of Data Integration Mastery and teaches both brands' courses, including GitHub Copilot certification (GH-300) preparation.

What Ari has created

  • AI Driven Development Methodology — a vendor-neutral methodology in which humans own intent, scope and decisions, and AI agents perform bounded work that is reviewed against evidence. Read the methodology reference →
  • AI Architect Mastery — an education brand for experienced developers who want a structured, production-minded way to work with AI agents.
  • Data Integration Mastery — the sister brand for integration architecture: what to build, while AI Architect Mastery teaches how to build it with AI agents. dataintegrationmastery.com →
  • GitHub Copilot Certification (GH-300) Preparation — a course on Udemy with video lectures, coding demos, written materials and a practice test. Course details →

Background

Ari has more than 25 years of experience in IT and has worked on hundreds of real-world projects, with a focus on software and integration architecture. His teaching comes from that practice: great software is built when human intent and machine execution meet through a clear structure.

How Ari works with AI

Human as Architect, AI as Builder. The human defines the goal, approves the plan and makes the final decisions. AI agents do the building within clear boundaries, and their work is accepted only after review, testing and evidence. Delegating execution does not delegate accountability.

Questions and answers

Who is Ari Vilkman?

Ari Vilkman is an AI Architect and the founder of AI Architect Mastery and Data Integration Mastery. He created the AI Driven Development Methodology and teaches GitHub Copilot and AI-assisted development to experienced developers.

Who created the AI Driven Development Methodology?

The AI Driven Development Methodology was created by Ari Vilkman, founder of AI Architect Mastery. It defines a human-led workflow of four phases, PRD → PLAN → TASK → IMPLEMENTATION, for building software with AI agents.

What is AI Architect Mastery?

AI Architect Mastery (AAM) is an education brand founded by Ari Vilkman. It teaches experienced developers the AI Driven Development Methodology and practical GitHub Copilot skills, including GH-300 certification preparation.

What is Data Integration Mastery?

Data Integration Mastery (DIM) is Ari Vilkman's sister brand for integration architecture. DIM covers what to build; AI Architect Mastery covers how to build it with AI agents.
