The AI Driven Development Methodology

Human as Architect → AI as Builder. Humans own intent, scope, architecture and final decisions; AI agents perform bounded work through PRD → PLAN → TASK → IMPLEMENTATION. Return to earlier steps when uncertainty, defects or changed understanding require it. Evidence informs human judgment; the workflow does not guarantee production readiness or authorize automatic release.

Read the AI Driven Development Methodology reference for the definition, responsibilities and four-phase workflow.
