Methodology/Human-led model

Human as Architect, AI as Builder

Human as Architect, AI as Builder means the human owns intent, constraints, delegation boundaries and acceptance, while AI agents research, propose, build and test within that direction. Delegating execution does not delegate accountability.

This is the responsibility view of AI Architect Mastery's AI Driven Development Methodology, also known as structured Vibe Coding. It applies across PRD → PLAN → TASK → IMPLEMENTATION, not only during code review.

Updated 2 October 2026 · Source: AAM Methodology v1.1, §§2–3 · Owner: AI Architect Mastery

Why keep responsibility explicit?

An agent can produce a coherent result without knowing which trade-offs a team is authorized to make. Explicit responsibility prevents a plausible implementation from silently becoming the product definition or architecture decision.

How the responsibility model works

  • The human defines: intended outcomes, non-goals, constraints and observable acceptance criteria.
  • The human delegates: one responsibility with selected context, permissions and a stop boundary.
  • The agent performs: approved research, planning, implementation or verification and reports evidence and limitations.
  • The human decides: whether the work is acceptable, needs revision, should be reassigned or must return to an earlier artifact.

The Human Architect is a responsibility, not necessarily a job title. A developer may hold it for a small change while consulting other owners for security or domain decisions.

Constructed decision: who chooses the policy?

An agent implementing an export endpoint finds two possible access policies. It can compare their implications, but it must not select a new business policy outside its assignment. The human clarifies the PRD, then authorizes the resulting bounded work.

Bounded autonomy, not constant interruption

Human leadership does not require approval for every microscopic action. An autonomy boundary defines which decisions may proceed independently and which require a stop. The checkpoint remains a meaningful decision boundary rather than a rubber stamp.

Related concepts

  • DEFINE → DELEGATE → BUILD → VERIFY translates responsibility into the memorable decision model.
  • Selected context and Agent Harness explain the environment in which the agent can act.
  • Human implementation checkpoint applies this responsibility to acceptance evidence.
